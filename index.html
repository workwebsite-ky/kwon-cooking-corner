Welcome to the corner

A real kitchen, a real baker, and nothing pre-made.

Kwon Cooking Corner started the way the best food businesses do — one tray at a time, for people who kept asking for more.

Founder Jakwon bakes every cookie, frosts every mini cake and preps every catering tray by hand. No factory mixes, no day-old inventory, no shortcuts. When you order a dozen strawberry crunch cookies, they come out of the oven for you — which is exactly why St. Pete keeps coming back.

Whether it's a birthday cake that has to look as good as it tastes, a graduation spread, or three dozen brownies for the office, K.C.C treats your order like it's going to their own table.

Frequently asked

For cookies, brownies and mini cakes, 48 hours is usually plenty. Custom celebration cakes and full catering orders are best booked 5–7 days ahead so the design and shopping can be planned properly. Got a last-minute occasion? Call or text — it's always worth asking.

Yes — delivery, pick-up and meet-up are all available around Saint Petersburg and the surrounding Tampa Bay area. Delivery is quoted based on distance, so send your address with your order and you'll get an exact figure up front.

Absolutely — that's most of what K.C.C does. Team colours, logos, gender reveals, graduations, character themes, hand-piped lettering. Send a photo or a Pinterest screenshot of what you have in mind and you'll get a quote and a realistic sketch of what's possible.

Catering is quoted per event rather than off a fixed price list, because headcount, menu and setup change everything. Tell us the date, the number of guests and roughly what you'd like to serve, and you'll get a clear written quote with no surprises.

Always mention allergies when you order. K.C.C bakes in a home kitchen that handles wheat, dairy, eggs, nuts and soy, so cross-contact can't be fully ruled out — but preferences and substitutions are accommodated wherever it's safely possible. Just ask before you book.
