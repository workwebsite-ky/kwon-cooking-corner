Meet Jakwon

Kwon Cooking Corner isn't a brand with a kitchen attached. It's one person who can genuinely cook, doing it for his own city.

Jakwon started baking for family and friends in Saint Petersburg — the kind of cookies people mention weeks later, the kind of birthday cake that ends up in everyone's photos. The orders kept coming, the requests got bigger, and what began at a home oven became Kwon Cooking Corner LLC.

He still makes every order himself. He still answers the phone himself. And he still refuses to send out anything he wouldn't happily put on his own table — which is the entire business model, and the reason it works.

Beyond the bakes, Jakwon cooks full catering spreads for parties, showers and community events across the Tampa Bay area, bringing the same standard to a forty-guest buffet as to a single box of cookies — backed by formal training in Professional Culinary Arts and Hospitality.

Trained & certified

Not just self-taught — qualified.

Jakwon holds a Certificate of Program Completion in Professional Culinary Arts and Hospitality from Pinellas Technical College.

The programme runs under standards approved by the Florida Department of Education, Division of Workforce Development, and he completed it on 19 June 2025. It covers professional kitchen practice, food safety and handling, and hospitality service — the things that separate a proper kitchen from a good home cook.

In practice, it means your order is prepared by someone formally trained in safe food handling, not just someone who bakes well. For a catering booking, that matters.

What drives us

Mission & vision

Our Mission

To make celebration food that's genuinely worth celebrating — baked fresh, priced fairly, and handled personally from the first message to the moment it reaches your table. No shortcuts, no shelf life, no treating a customer's birthday like a transaction.

Our Vision

To become the name Saint Petersburg reaches for whenever there's something to celebrate — growing from a home kitchen into a full local operation, while keeping the thing that made people order in the first place: real food, made by someone who cares how it turns out.
