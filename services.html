Service 01

Fresh-Baked Cakes & Treats

Hand-finished bundt cakes, cupcakes, cheesecake and cookies, baked in small batches the day your order goes out.

This is where Kwon Cooking Corner started. Oreo mini bundt cakes under a white chocolate drizzle, Cookie Monster cupcakes piled with blue buttercream, Fruity Pebbles cheesecake, red velvet pound cake by the slice, fudge brownies, and the cookies that built the following in the first place.

Order a single box for the house, a dozen for the office, or a full tray for a party — the price per piece stays the same, and so does the quality.

  • Oreo mini bundt cakes & Fruity Pebbles cheesecake
  • Red velvet cupcakes & Cookie Monster cupcakes, frosted by hand
  • Fudge brownies and cookie-butter dessert bars
  • Boxed for gifting, trayed for events

Service 02

Custom Celebration Cakes

Send a photo, a colour or just an idea. It comes back as a cake that looks like the occasion it was made for.

Birthdays, graduations, gender reveals, anniversaries, team parties and church events — K.C.C builds the design around your theme rather than pulling one off a catalogue page. Team logos, hand-piped script, tie-dye buttercream, drip finishes and sculpted toppers are all on the table.

Every cake is baked to order, filled and frosted fresh, and finished the day it leaves the kitchen — so it tastes like a cake, not like a display piece.

  • Themed designs, logos and character cakes
  • Hand-piped lettering and custom colour matching
  • Single-tier, layered and sheet cakes
  • Matching cupcakes and treat boxes available

Service 03

Party & Event Catering

Jakwon cooks as well as he bakes. When you're feeding a room, K.C.C handles the whole spread.

Birthday parties, graduations, baby showers, repasts, church functions, office lunches and family reunions — catering is built around your headcount and your menu, not a fixed package someone else designed.

Tell us the date, how many people and roughly what you'd like to serve. You'll get a clear written quote covering the food, the trays and the drop-off, so there's nothing to work out on the day.

  • Hot food trays cooked to order
  • Dessert and cookie trays for the sweet table
  • Portions planned to your guest count
  • Delivery, pick-up or meet-up across St. Pete
