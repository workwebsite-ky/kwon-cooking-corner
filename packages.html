Before you order

Pricing questions

Cupcakes go out by the pack rather than one at a time: $10 for a pack of 6, or $25 for a dozen. That applies to the Cookie Monster and red velvet cupcakes alike. Mixed packs are fine — just say which flavours you want when you order.

For the signature treats there's no strict minimum — you can order a single box. For catering and full dessert tables a minimum applies based on the menu, and it will always be stated clearly in your quote before you commit to anything.

Because an honest number depends on the details. A two-tier cake with a hand-piped logo and a simple single-tier cake take very different amounts of work, and catering for 15 guests is nothing like catering for 90. Rather than publish a number that would be wrong for most people, K.C.C quotes each order properly — for free.

Payment details are confirmed when your order is booked. Larger custom cakes and catering orders normally take a deposit to secure the date, with the balance due on completion — all of which is set out in your quote up front.

Delivery is quoted based on your distance from Saint Petersburg, so it's included in your written quote rather than added as a surprise. Pick-up and meet-up options are always free.

Usually yes, as long as there's still time before baking starts. Get in touch as soon as you know — the earlier the change, the easier it is to accommodate.
